Why we're doing this

Starting a business is common in the UK, but keeping one going is harder. About 38% of UK businesses started in 2019 were still trading five years later (ONS), and the Enterprise Research Centre found that just 47% of start-ups registered in 2020 survived three years (ERC).

The same researchers describe UK business support as fragmented and patchy, and say many firms only seek advice once they're in trouble. Meanwhile, skilled freelancers and providers struggle to reach the businesses that need them.

These figures don't tell us why businesses close, so we're asking small businesses and providers directly what gets in the way of finding each other.
